## About Us

**Takumi Association** is a community-driven initiative dedicated to making **Japanese language education** and **IT skills** accessible to everyone.

Our mission is to empower learners by providing quality educational resources, practical learning opportunities, and a supportive community—regardless of their background or financial situation.

## 🎯 Our Mission

* 🇯🇵 Make Japanese language learning free and accessible.
* 💻 Provide practical IT education and career guidance.
* 🌏 Build an inclusive learning community.
* 🚀 Help learners grow through real-world projects and collaboration.

## 🌟 What We Do

* 📚 Japanese language learning resources
* 💻 IT courses and learning materials
* 🌐 Open-source educational projects
* 🤝 Community events and workshops
* 🎓 Career guidance and mentorship

## 🤝 Contributing

We welcome contributions from educators, developers, designers, translators, and volunteers.

You can help by:

* Improve documentation
* Fix bugs and add new features
* Create educational content
* Translate learning materials
* Share ideas and feedback

## 💙 Our Values

* Education for Everyone
* Open Knowledge
* Continuous Learning
* Collaboration
* Community First
